2. Your rights if goods are faulty

Irish consumers may have a short-term right to terminate within 30 days of receiving non-conforming goods, or may request repair or replacement; further remedies may apply depending on the circumstances. We will not require a consumer to pay necessary return costs when the law makes us responsible for a faulty-goods return. Please contact us first so we can discuss a practical collection or reimbursement arrangement, especially for international returns.

3. Change-of-mind returns

This is a different procedure. Most online purchases may be cancelled within 14 days after receipt without a reason, subject to legal exceptions. Please read our Right of Withdrawal for the notice, return deadline, refund and return-cost rules.

5. Statutory rights remain intact
