You can notify us that you wish to exercise your statutory right to cancel a distance purchase without providing a reason, generally within 14 days after receipt of the goods, subject to statutory exceptions.

Send a clear statement including your name, order number or other order details, and the goods you wish to cancel to info@stepar.ie. You may also use the optional model cancellation form. Email and postal notification remain available even if an optional online order-lookup form does not work.

We will acknowledge your message. The deadline is met when you send your cancellation notice within the applicable period. After notifying us, you normally have another 14 days to return the goods to the Czech return address. See our Right of Withdrawal for full details.
