3. Purposes and lawful bases

We process order, payment, shipping, returns and pre-contract enquiries on the basis of contract performance and pre-contract steps (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). Accounting, taxation and other mandatory records use Article 6(1)(c). Security, abuse prevention, operational troubleshooting, defence of legal claims and customer assistance outside a specific contract rely on legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)), namely keeping the shop secure, reliable and contactable, balanced against individuals' rights. Where optional analytics or advertising tracking is used, the legal basis is consent (Article 6(1)(a)) and the applicable Irish ePrivacy rules. Consent may be withdrawn prospectively. We do not use solely automated decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects in ordinary order processing.

7. How long information is retained

Order, invoice and transaction records are kept for the periods required by applicable Czech accounting and tax law. Account information is retained while the account is active and as otherwise needed for statutory records; upon a valid request for closure, data not subject to an overriding retention ground will be deleted or anonymised. Technical security logs are kept only for proportionate security needs, and optional analytics data follow the retention settings stated in the relevant consent/cookie information. We periodically review these criteria and do not retain personal information indefinitely without a purpose.
