Your right to cancel

You have the right to cancel most distance contracts for goods within 14 days without giving a reason. The cancellation period expires 14 days after the day on which you, or a person nominated by you other than the carrier, takes physical possession of the goods. For several goods ordered together but delivered separately, the period runs from receipt of the last good; for a good delivered in several lots or pieces, from the last lot or piece.

Refunds

If you cancel, we will reimburse all payments received for the cancelled goods, including the cost of the least expensive standard delivery method offered for the order (but not any supplementary cost of a more expensive delivery choice). Reimbursement will be made without undue delay and within 14 days after we are informed of the cancellation. We may withhold reimbursement until we receive the returned goods or you supply evidence that you have sent them back, whichever is earlier. We will use the same means of payment as the original transaction unless expressly agreed otherwise, without imposing reimbursement fees.

Returning goods

You must send the goods to ŠTĚPAŘ s.r.o., Střítežská 570, 572 01 Polička, Czech Republic, without undue delay and no later than 14 days after informing us of cancellation. The deadline is met if they are dispatched before that period ends. For a change-of-mind cancellation, you bear the direct cost of return postage. The cost of returning unusually bulky goods which cannot normally be returned by post must be disclosed or reasonably estimated before the order; contact us before ordering if this is relevant. You are liable only for diminished value caused by handling beyond what is necessary to establish the nature, characteristics and functioning of goods.

Exceptions

Statutory exceptions are interpreted narrowly. They can include goods made to a consumer's specifications or clearly personalised; goods liable to deteriorate or expire rapidly; and sealed goods not suitable for return for health-protection or hygiene reasons if unsealed after delivery. These exceptions do not automatically apply to all medical devices, cosmetics, herbs, teas or supplements, and opening an outer shipping carton does not by itself remove the right to cancel. Other specific exceptions apply where provided by law.
