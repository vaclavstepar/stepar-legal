Payment

Payment on www.stepar.ie is offered only through GoPay. The payment options available inside GoPay and the total to be paid are presented before the customer confirms an order.

Shipping

For goods orders of €200 or more, standard shipping is free. Below €200, available carriers, delivery charges and estimated delivery times are shown at checkout before the order is placed. We do not advertise a particular delivery price or carrier for Ireland unless it is offered in the checkout. Any additional charge must be shown before purchase.

Unless a different period is agreed, delivery should take place without undue delay and no later than 30 days after contract formation. If delivery fails, statutory remedies apply.
